Game Details

How to play

You always play offense. The computer always plays defense. Home drives left to right; Visitor drives right to left. With two players, pass the game over when possession changes.

  • Lights: the blinking bright light is the ball carrier, steady bright lights are your blockers and receiver, dim lights are the defense.
  • Run: set the selector to Run/Kick. The carrier and two blockers move together, one step per key press. A blocker that hits a tackler takes him out, and both disappear.
  • Pass: set the selector to Pass. Only the receiver moves. Step him up into the quarterback's row, then press K/P. Throw while he is out of line and a deep defender intercepts.
  • Kick: on 4th down only, in Run/Kick, press K/P before moving. A missed field goal is a punt.
  • Yardage: your first two steps forward only cancel a 2-yard loss. The window is 9 yards and wraps around, so keep running.
  • After every play: the ball carrier blinks alone where he went down. Hold D to see down, yards to go and field position, then release to line up the next play. Hold D again to see the score and clock.
  • Scoring: touchdown 7, field goal 3, safety 2. Four quarters of 15 game minutes; the clock runs only during a play.
