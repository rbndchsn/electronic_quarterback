The basics

  • You always play offense. The computer always plays defense. You never control the defense.
  • There are two teams, Home and Visitor. When the ball changes hands, you (or a friend) play offense for the other team.
  • Home drives left to right. Visitor drives right to left.
  • You have four downs to gain ten yards. Gain them and you get four more.

Reading the screen

The screen is a 9-yard window of the field, four rows deep. Each player is one red light.

  • Bright and blinking: the player with the ball.
  • Bright and steady: your blockers and your receiver.
  • Dim: the defense. All defenders look the same.

Holding D replaces the field with numbers. The first hold shows the top line of labels, the next hold shows the bottom line:

ReadingMeaning
3 9 u 253rd down, 9 yards to go, ball on your own 25 (75 yards from a touchdown).
3 9 d 253rd down, 9 yards to go, ball on the defense's 25 (25 yards from a touchdown).
14 11.9 07Home 14, 11.9 minutes left in the quarter, Visitor 7.

u means you are on your own side of the 50 and the number counts up as you advance. d means you are on the defense's side and it counts down.

How a play goes

  1. Set the Run/Kick · Pass switch. The formation appears.
  2. Press any move key. The play starts, the clock runs and the defense comes at you.
  3. The play ends with a whistle. Only the ball carrier is left on screen, blinking where he went down.
  4. Hold D to read the new down and field position, then release. The teams line up again.

Nothing happens after a play until you hold and release D.

Running plays

Run formation, Home team. The ball carrier blinks at the left; his two blockers are one step ahead, one row up and one row down.

  • The ball carrier and both blockers move together, one step per key press.
  • A blocker and a tackler that meet are both removed from the field.
  • A tackler that reaches the ball carrier ends the play. So does running into one.
  • The window is only 9 yards. Run off the far edge and you come back in at the near edge, still on the same play.

Tip: a tackler starts straight ahead of you, so running straight gains almost nothing. Change rows so that a blocker, not you, meets him.

Passing plays

Pass formation, Home team. The quarterback blinks at the left with a blocker in front of him. The receiver is the bright light one row below the blocker.

  • The quarterback and his blocker do not move. You move only the receiver.
  • The receiver cannot be tackled, and he cannot step into a cell that another player is in.
  • Four defenders (five on skill 2) rush the quarterback. The two deepest defenders cover the receiver.
  • Press K/P to throw at any time. The ball flies straight along the quarterback's row.
  • Only three players can catch it: your receiver and the two deep defenders. The nearest of them in that row gets it.
When you throwResult
Receiver is in the quarterback's row, with no deep defender between themComplete. He becomes the ball carrier and you run with him.
A deep defender is in that row, closer to the quarterback than your receiverIntercepted. The other team takes over at that spot.
Receiver is not in that row, and a deep defender isIntercepted.
Nobody who can catch is in that rowIncomplete. You lose the down; the ball does not move.
A rusher reaches the quarterback before you throwSack: a 2-yard loss.

A good moment to throw. The receiver has looped under the rush and stepped up into the quarterback's row. The deep defender is behind him, on the goal-line side, so the pass is complete. The dim light between quarterback and receiver is a rusher, who cannot catch the ball.

Tips:

  • Never throw at the snap. The receiver starts out of line, so the ball goes to a deep defender.
  • The quickest completion is forward once, up once, then K/P. It is a short catch, and you still need to run.
  • The deep defenders follow the receiver from behind. Do not run him past them, or they will be in front of him when you throw.
  • A rusher standing in the quarterback's row cannot catch the ball. It passes him.

Kicking

  • You can kick on 4th down only, with the switch on Run/Kick. Press K/P before you move.
  • The closer you are to the goal line, the better the chance of a field goal. From far away it cannot be made.
  • A kick that is not a field goal is a punt. The other team takes over farther downfield.

Yardage and downs

Every play starts 2 yards in the hole. Your first two steps forward only cancel that.

Each further crossing on the same play9 more yards
  • On a pass, the receiver's distance from the line when he catches it counts the same way, and then every step he runs adds a yard.
  • Gain 10 yards within four downs for a new first down.
  • Fall short on 4th down and the other team takes over where the play ended.

Scoring and kickoffs

ScorePointsHow
Touchdown7Carry the ball past the goal line. No end zone is drawn; the tune tells you.
Field goal3A successful 4th-down kick.
Safety2You lose yardage into your own end zone. The other team gets the points and the ball at its 20.

After a score the kickoff happens off screen. Usually the receiving team starts first-and-ten at its own 20. Now and then the kick is short, and the first play is a run back.

Clock and quarters

  • A game is four quarters of 15 game minutes. These are not real minutes.
  • The clock runs only while a play is live.
  • When a quarter ends, the score and time appear by themselves. Hold and release D to go on.
  • Home receives to start the game. Visitor receives to start the second half.
  • After the fourth quarter the game locks on the final score. Switch off, then on, for a new game.

Where the rules come from

Emu Retro Football is an original, free, fan-made game. Its rules follow the published instructions of a 1978 LED handheld football game. It uses no code, artwork, name or logo from that product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any toy maker.

Those instructions do not say how fast or how cleverly the defense moves, the odds of a field goal, how far a punt goes, or how fast the clock runs. Those parts are estimates and may be adjusted as the game is tuned.
